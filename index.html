Cinematic Vision

Cinema Is Cinema
Only When It Is Wide.

Every Mobai Cinema original is shot, edited, and mastered in widescreen formats — purpose-built for the mobile screens that increasingly dominate how India watches. We do not compromise on the cinematic experience.

2.39 : 1
2.39 : 1 — CinemaScope
Our Signature Format — Theatrical Widescreen
The same aspect ratio used in Hollywood's most cinematic productions — Interstellar, Dune, The Revenant. Mobai Cinema originals are the first Indian short films shot exclusively in 2.39:1 for mobile. Black letterbox bars are intentional — they signal cinema, not YouTube. When a user sees those bars on their phone, they know they are watching something serious.

21 : 9 — Foldable Display
Fold-Native — Samsung Z Fold & OnePlus Open
When a Samsung Z Fold is fully unfolded, its display ratio is approximately 22:9 — the closest consumer screen to true CinemaScope. Mobai Cinema originals mastered in 21:9 fill this screen edge to edge with zero black bars. This is a content gap no OTT has addressed. We are the exclusive content partner for foldable-native cinema in India.
